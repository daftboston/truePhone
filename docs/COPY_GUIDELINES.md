# TruePhone Content & Copy Guidelines

Version 1.0

---

# Purpose

This document defines how TruePhone communicates with users.

Every word inside the application should reinforce the brand.

The way we write is as important as the way we design.

Users should feel:

- Safe
- Informed
- Respected
- Confident
- Never manipulated

If the interface builds trust visually, the copy builds trust emotionally.

# Writing Principles

## Be Clear

Always choose the clearest wording.

Users should understand every sentence the first time they read it.

---

## Be Honest

Never exaggerate.

Never overpromise.

Never create false urgency.

---

Bad

"This is the safest marketplace in the world!"

Good

"Every listing is manually reviewed before publication."

---

## Be Human

Write like a knowledgeable person speaking to another person.

Avoid sounding like software.

---

Bad

"Your request has been successfully processed."

Good

"Your listing has been submitted for review."

---

## Be Short

Short sentences.

Simple words.

No unnecessary explanations.

---

Bad

"You have successfully completed the listing creation process."

Good

"Your listing is ready for review."

---

## Be Helpful

Every message should answer:

What happened?

What does it mean?

What should I do next?

# Transparency Rules

Never hide fees.

Never hide limitations.

Never hide waiting times.

Never hide review decisions.

Always explain why.

---

# Reading Level

Write for a general audience.

Avoid technical language whenever possible.

If technical information is necessary, explain it.

---

Bad

"Activation Lock detected."

Good

"This iPhone is still linked to an Apple Account and can't be sold until Activation Lock is removed."

---

# Buttons

Buttons should begin with a verb.

Good

Browse iPhones

Continue

Buy Now

Save Changes

Send Message

Submit Listing

Review Listing

---

Avoid

Click Here

Proceed

Execute

Confirm Operation

---

# Empty States

Every empty state should include:

A clear title

A brief explanation

A primary action

---

Example

Title

No favorites yet

Description

Save listings you're interested in so you can find them later.

Button

Browse iPhones

---

# Error Messages

Structure

What happened

↓

Why

↓

What to do next

---

Example

We couldn't upload your photos.

Please check your internet connection and try again.

---

Avoid

Unknown Error

Unexpected Exception

500 Error

Request Failed

---

# Validation Messages

Be specific.

Bad

Invalid input.

Good

Battery health must be between 1% and 100%.

---

# Notifications

Notifications should be concise.

Examples

Your listing has been approved.

You received a new message.

Your payment has been confirmed.

Your order has been completed.

---

# Confirmation Messages

Explain what happens next.

Example

Your listing has been submitted.

Our review team will review it before it becomes public.

---

# Review Rejections

Always be respectful.

Never sound accusatory.

Example

Your listing needs a few updates before it can be published.

Please review the notes below and submit it again.

---

# Loading Messages

Keep them short.

Examples

Loading listings...

Uploading photos...

Searching...

Preparing your checkout...

---

# Success Messages

Celebrate modestly.

Examples

Listing approved.

Payment received.

Message sent.

Purchase completed.

Avoid excessive excitement.

---

Bad

🎉 Amazing!!

Your order is AWESOME!!

---

Good

Your purchase is confirmed.

Seller

Buyer

Reviewer

Verified Seller

Order

Favorite

---

---

# Date & Time

Use the Colombian Spanish format in the application.

Example

8 July 2026

2:45 PM

Future localization should follow the user's locale.

---

# Currency

Always display COP.

Example

COP $2,350,000

Never omit the currency.

---

# Numbers

Use separators consistently.

Example

2,350,000

Not

2350000

---

# Email Tone

Professional.

Helpful.

Concise.

Every email should answer:

Why am I receiving this?

What happened?

What should I do next?

---

# Support Tone

Assume the user is frustrated.

Remain calm.

Never argue.

Never blame.

Always acknowledge the issue.

Always offer the next step.

---

# AI Writing Rules

Whenever AI generates text for TruePhone:

Prefer clarity over creativity.

Prefer confidence over excitement.

Prefer honesty over marketing.

Prefer simplicity over cleverness.

Every sentence should make the marketplace feel more trustworthy.

---

# Final Rule

Every word inside TruePhone should answer one question:

"Does this make the user feel more confident?"

If not—

rewrite it.
